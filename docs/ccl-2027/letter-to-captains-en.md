**Subject:** CCL 2027 — Preparing Local Online Qualifiers

Dear Captains,

The Carcassonne Champions League 2027 Qualification Stage begins on **November 16** — less than 11 weeks from now.

Please [complete this form](https://forms.gle/WLDo2gjjR7UHDjCJ9) as soon as possible so that we can understand the current status of player selection in your association. We kindly ask you not to delay.

Below you will find detailed information about the key dates, selection rules, and information we need from you.

## Key Dates

Qualification Stage

- **November 11** — registration deadline
- **November 16** — start of the Qualification Stage

Main Stage

- **December 30** — registration deadline
- **January 4, 2027** — start of the Main Stage

## Reminder: How Players Should Be Selected

- **Qualification Stage:** An association may nominate players selected through a local tournament or by another method. The selection process must be clear to the association's community.
- **Main Stage:** Players must be selected based on the results of a local **online championship or online qualifier**.

Additional criteria:

- Results from an in-person national championship cannot be used as a selection criterion for the CCL Main Stage.
- Only in exceptional circumstances may the CCL organizers approve a player's participation in the Main Stage without a local online qualifier. The association must provide valid reasons in advance and obtain separate approval from the CCL organizers.

## Association Ranking and Allocation of Places

The current ranking is available on the [Association Ranking 2026](https://carcassonne.gg/Association-Ranking-2026/) page.

The final ranking will be determined after the following tournaments have concluded:

- Asian Cup 2026;
- ETCOC 2026;
- King of Carcassonne.

The allocation rules remain unchanged from the previous season:

| Association ranking | Main Stage | Qualification Stage |
| ---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1–4 | 2 places | 1 place |
| 5–12 | 1 place | 2 places |
| 13–32 | 1 place | 1 place |
| 33–40 | — | 2 places |
| 41–56 | — | 1 place |

As the ranking may still change, the final number of places allocated to each association may also change.

## Information We Need from You

We ask all captains to [complete the form](https://forms.gle/WLDo2gjjR7UHDjCJ9) as soon as possible. Please do not delay.

If your association **has already held a local online tournament**, please provide:

- the name of and link to the tournament or tournaments;
- the method that will be used to select players for the CCL 2027 Main Stage and Qualification Stage.

If your association **has not yet held a local online tournament**, please provide:

- the planned start and end dates;
- the organizer's name and contact details;
- the method that will be used to select players for the CCL 2027 Main Stage and Qualification Stage.

Player registration will take place later.

We understand that the final ranking and the number of places allocated to individual associations may still change. At this stage, it is sufficient to provide a preliminary plan and explain how it will be adapted if your association receives more or fewer places.

## How to Add Your Championship to carcassonne.gg

If you decide to hold a championship, we can add it to carcassonne.gg. Open the [Tournament Details](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gurJ5y59_szlao1Ed0tW8HSQZ8r4eYxq9twkS6RutXM/edit?usp=sharing) spreadsheet, create your own copy by selecting **File → Make a copy**, fill in the tournament details, and send the completed copy to us.

## Recommended Championship Formats

For associations that are still planning their tournament, we have also prepared separate recommendations on championship formats. A PDF containing these recommendations is attached to this email. It includes options for different numbers of participants and different amounts of time available.
